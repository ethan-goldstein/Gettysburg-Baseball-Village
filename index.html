The Village

Built for the way baseball summers should feel.

Gettysburg Baseball Village brings teams from across the region to one of America's most storied towns for weekends of real competition. Well-kept fields, organized schedules and a staff that runs on baseball, with the rolling hills of Adams County on the horizon.

Come for the tournament. Leave with the best weekend of the summer.

  • Tournament-ready fields

    Groomed infields and schedules that run on time.

  • Built for families

    Shade, concessions and room to spread out between games.

  • Walk to everything

    Fields, dorms and dining sit close together. Downtown is minutes away.

  • Run by baseball people

    Coaches and former players who know what a great weekend looks like.

Team Housing

Stay on campus. Stay together.

Players and coaches stay in Gettysburg College residence halls: a home base that keeps the whole team under one roof, just minutes from the fields.

  1. 01

    Safe

    Secure college residence halls with coaches right down the hall.

  2. 02

    Affordable

    Team rates that beat a hotel block, with no shuttling between rooms.

  3. 03

    Close to the fields

    Minutes from the diamond, dining and downtown Gettysburg.

Visit Gettysburg

Between games, there's a whole town to explore.

History on every corner, good food on every block, and plenty to keep younger siblings happy.

Explore

Gettysburg National Military Park

Walk the fields of 1863, then catch the film and Cyclorama at the Museum & Visitor Center.

Downtown

Lincoln Square

Shops, sweets and history in the heart of town, a short walk from campus.

Eat

Dobbin House Tavern

Team dinner in a 1776 stone house on Steinwehr Avenue, a few blocks from Lincoln Square.

Family fun

Mister Ed's Elephant Museum

Thousands of elephants plus a candy emporium kids won't forget, a short drive west on Route 30.
